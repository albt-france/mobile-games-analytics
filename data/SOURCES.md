# Data sources

## Real, public data
| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `clean/rovio_quarterly_kpis.csv` | Rovio Entertainment interim reports Q1-2021, Q3-2021, Q1-2022, Q3-2022 and financial statement bulletins 2021 and 2022 (rovio.com investor archive). Q2 figures for UA and revenue are derived as full year minus the three published quarters. |
| `clean/rovio_gross_bookings_by_game.csv` | Same reports, "Game-specific gross bookings" tables. "Other games" = total minus the listed titles (includes AB Pop!). |
| `raw/players/cookie_cats.csv` | Public "Cookie Cats" A/B test dataset (Tactile Entertainment, 90,189 players, gate at level 30 vs 40). |

Notes: from Q3-2021, Rovio's "all games" KPIs include Ruby Games (hyper-casual), which inflates DAU/MAU and lowers ARPDAU. The analysis uses the "top 5 games" KPIs for trends.
`ua_share_of_gross_bookings` divides UA investment by games' gross bookings; Rovio itself reports UA as a share of games' revenue, so values differ slightly.

## Simulated data (clearly labelled)
`clean/sim_*.csv` is a **simulated** player-level dataset for a casual mobile puzzle game, generated by `src/generate_sim.py` (fixed random seed).
It is calibrated on public benchmarks: D1/D7 retention from Cookie Cats, top-5 ARPDAU (~EUR 0.20) and payer ratio (MUP/MAU ~2%) from Rovio's reports, and typical CPIs by channel.
It exists to demonstrate cohort, LTV forecasting and ROAS methods. It is **not** Rovio data.
